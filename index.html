ATS Optimization Tips

  • Use standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
  • Include keywords from the job description
  • Quantify achievements with numbers and metrics
  • Avoid graphics, tables, or unusual formatting
  • Use a clean, single-column layout
  • Save as PDF or DOCX format

What is ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers to filter resumes before they reach human reviewers. Optimizing your resume for ATS increases your chances of getting noticed.
